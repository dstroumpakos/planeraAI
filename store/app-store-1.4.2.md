## English (U.S.)

### Promotional Text
```
New: an inbox that keeps every deal alert in one place, save whole countries to your wishlist, and a little celebration when your trip is ready.
```

### What's New in This Version
```
• Notification inbox: every deal alert and trip update is now saved in the app, so you can find it later even if you swiped it away. A badge shows what you haven't read yet.
• Deal alerts that matter: price-drop and deal alerts now only cover flights from your home airport.
• Save whole countries: add "Spain" or "Japan" to your wishlist and we'll look for deals across that country's main airports.
• Fresh animations: new loading screens, plus a small celebration when your trip is ready or Premium is unlocked.
• Bug fixes and performance improvements.
```
