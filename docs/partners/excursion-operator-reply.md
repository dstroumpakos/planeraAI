# Partner reply — excursion / activity operators

Template for answering an operator who replied to the `partners@planeraai.app`
outreach asking "can we have more information?".

Rules of the template:

- **Reply in the language they wrote in.** Greek operators write Greek; the
  English block goes underneath so a colleague forwarded the thread can read it.
- **No honorific.** Greek honorifics are gendered and a first name does not tell
  you which one to use — open with `Καλησπέρα σας,` and address the department.
- **One ask per email.** A 15-minute call, with "send us your programme and
  price list" as the low-effort fallback. Not both as equals, not three.
- **Claim only what ships.** The app plans day-by-day itineraries and surfaces
  activities inside them. Do not promise instant confirmation, a live
  availability feed, or a listing date until the connector work is agreed.
- **Numbers are a decision, not a template.** Commission rate stays a bracket
  until it is signed off; never send an unreviewed percentage.

## What to have ready before the call

| Question they will ask | Answer to give |
| --- | --- |
| How many travellers will actually see us? | Give real current numbers or say we are early and offer launch terms — never an invented figure. |
| What commission? | The agreed bracket, net-rate or gross-minus-commission, whichever the operator prefers to quote in. |
| Who takes the booking? | Today: we hand the traveller to you (link / request). A direct booking flow is a later step, not a promise on this call. |
| When do we go live? | After we have the programme, prices and availability, plus one round of listing review with them. |
| Do we need an API? | No. Operators of this size are onboarded from their own PDF/spreadsheet; the supplier connectors in `convex/agency/connectors/` are for wholesalers and aggregators. |

## After the call

- Log the operator, contact and outcome wherever partner pipeline lives.
- If they send a price list, keep it out of the repo — it is commercial
  third-party material.
